PHYSICS × INTELLIGENCE

Hi, I'm Yiming.

I am a third-year undergraduate in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at Wuhan University. I am interested in how intelligent systems perceive, model, and interact with the physical world.

My current work focuses on occupancy world models and camera–radar perception. I also explore AI for materials science and build agent-based tools for research, education, and creative applications.

Research interestsPhysical AI · World Models · Multimodal Perception · AI for Science
